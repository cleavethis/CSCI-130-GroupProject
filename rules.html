How to Play Wordle Clone🤔💭

Rules📋:

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

How to Play

  1. Enter a 5-letter word and submit your guess.
  2. The color of the tiles will change to indicate how close your guess was to the hidden word.
  3. Green indicates the letter is in the correct position, yellow indicates the letter is in the word but in the wrong position, and gray indicates the letter is not in the word at all.
  4. You have 6 tries to guess the hidden word correctly.
  5. If you guess the word correctly within 6 tries, you win! If not, you lose and can try again.
A

Green: Correct letter in the correct position

B

Yellow: Correct letter in the wrong position

C

Gray: Letter is not in the word at all

Repeated Letters🔁

If the hidden word contains a repeated letter, you will see the letter highlighted in yellow for each occurrence.

Scoring💯

You earn points based on how quickly you guess the word. The faster you guess it, the more points you earn.

End of the Game🎉

The game ends when you either guess the word correctly or exhaust all 6 attempts.
